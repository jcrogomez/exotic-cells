# MISFIT-02: a drawn cell of known time, read by the counter

Run on 2026-11-17 against a pre-registration written before the cell was drawn
([`MISFIT-02_2026-11-17_celda_de_tiempo.md`](../../cells/preregistros/MISFIT-02_2026-11-17_celda_de_tiempo.md)). It starts from
a reference bundle by an outside assistant (question, negative controls, extraction levels); its 30 and 60 pF capacitors
do not fit a tile and its capture module would have been a second architecture, so the cell and the counter are ours.
**Layout, extraction and simulation. No silicon.**

The question: can the instrument's counter recover an interval set by the geometry of a hand-drawn cell, and can what the
cell contributes be told from what the instrument contributes?

## The cell

A node precharged through a pfet and discharged through a weak nfet (0.42/24), loaded by a MOS capacitor (12 × 12 µm): one on
side A, two on side B, each read by an inverter. 13 devices, 73 × 20 µm, generated by [`cell/gen_tcell.py`](cell/gen_tcell.py).
It has no enable and no source select: it is a control of the measuring chain, not a full slot.

## The chain is tested in two legs, not co-simulated

1. SPICE of the cell extracted from its GDS gives the instant of the edge.
2. The instrument's RTL, with that instant in its slot model and the clock phase swept, gives the count.

A small transistor-level run covers the joint between them (the slow edge into a buffer and two flip-flops). Simulating the
whole tile at transistor level through one acquisition is out of reach here. Nothing below is a co-simulation.

## What the instrument adds, and what the cell adds

- **Quantisation:** one count is 20 ns: 3.7 % of A, 1.9 % of B. A single reading cannot do better.
- **Capture latency:** between two and three clocks, depending on phase; a fixed offset of two is subtracted, so a reading is
  biased by up to one clock on top of quantisation ([`results/counts.txt`](results/counts.txt)).
- **Supply:** 10 mV moves the cell 0.86 %, under one count for A. The instrument does not see it in one reading; the XC-19
  channel moves 6.5 % for the same 10 mV.
- **Temperature and process** move the time by tens of per cent and the ratio by 0.6 %: on this cell the ratio is the robust
  observable, and the absolute time is a thermometer.
- **Slow edge:** the output takes 19 to 29 ns to rise, about one clock period. It went through the buffer and flops cleanly in
  the 20 phases tried.

## Stage 2: the real instrument core and the cell in one tile

The instrument's own core (`rtl/misfit_core.v`, four slots of logic, slot 0 brought out on three pins) hardened apart, the cell
below it, by the route of arm B of MISFIT-01 ([`tile/`](tile/)).

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Core hardening | **First attempt failed**: in 161 × 70.72 µm the core is at 80.6 % utilisation and detailed placement fails. Second attempt, 161 × 87.04 µm: passes, 1047 cells, setup slack 3.6 ns at 20 ns, 84.8 % with fill |
| Assembly | **First attempt wrong**: the cell was placed by the corner of its bounding box instead of its origin and landed on the core (35 DRC errors, every net of the cell shorted). Second attempt: DRC 0 on the GDS read back |
| LVS, tile extracted from its GDS | "Circuits match uniquely": the core's cells and the 13 devices of the cell | 
| Precheck | Passes (15 checks) on the first run of the corrected tile |
| Controls | Supply not joined, and cell left out: LVS fails both; DRC is 0 in both |

**What stage 2 found that matters for MISFIT:** the instrument's digital core alone takes about 10 800 µm² of cells with fill,
most of a 1×1 tile (17 950 µm²), leaving a strip 24 µm high for cells. Four slots with free-geometry cells do not fit a 1×1.

## Not shown

- Any co-simulation of cell and instrument; the count is the RTL's answer to SPICE's time.
- The cell's timing inside the tile: the times are of the cell alone, the same GDS, with an assumed 10 fF load.
- A metastability rate; noise; mismatch between the two sides.
- That the cell obeys the slot contract: it has no enable and no source select.
- Silicon.
