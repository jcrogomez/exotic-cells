# `xc19_channel`: one complete XC-19 channel as a single cell

The bias cell and two identical readout cells (device A and its twin B), wired on metal 1. Ports: VDD, GND, C1, PREA, PREB, XA,
XB. 25.5 x 5.1 um, about 130 um2. Generated by `xc19_channel.tcl` from the two cells next to this folder.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Magic DRC, full rule set, whole cell | 0 violations (after joining the two precharge wells, which violated the well spacing rule) |
| KLayout `sky130A_mr.drc` | 0 violations |
| Extraction | 23 devices: 16 mirror, 3 chain, 2 devices under test, 2 precharge. The intended circuit |
| Residual | 18 minimum nfets all at `sa = sb = 0.30` |
| Simulation, extracted and annotated, three in series, 133 fF ideal per node (`channel_sim.sp`) | A and B discharge in 29.20 us and agree to 1 part in 100 000 |
| Supply, same simulation | one stage alone moves 6.5 % for 10 mV; the A/B ratio moves 0.0001 % (no mismatch in this run) |

Exploratory (`explore_moscap.sp`): a 4 x 4 um nfet used as the added capacitor gives 27.1 us against 29.2 us with the ideal
133 fF, so a transistor of 16 um2 can play that part. Not drawn yet.

## What this is not

Not a macro yet. Missing: the added capacitors and the bias filter capacitor as layout, pins on a routing grid, an abstract for
placement, and the decision on how the cell gets its supply inside a digital design (real power pins, or supply through
ordinary signals as our earlier passive macros did). Until then the wire from X to its inverter is not in any number here.
No mismatch, no noise: those are in [`experiments/xc19_noise`](../../experiments/xc19_noise/README.md) and the Monte Carlo of
`experiments/xc19_weak_inversion`.
