# `xc19_read`: readout cell for XC-19

First layout of the project that is meant to become a macro. One minimum `nfet_01v8` (0.42/0.15 um, 0.30 um of diffusion on
each side) discharges node X; a `pfet_01v8_hvt` of 0.42/0.50 um precharges it. Ports on local interconnect: X, VB, PRE, VDD, GND.
About 2.3 x 3.7 um. Generated by `xc19_read.tcl`.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|

| KLayout `sky130A_mr.drc` | 0 violations |
| KLayout full deck (`sky130A.lydrc`) with front-end checks switched on | 0 violations |
| Extraction | the two intended devices, body nodes on GND and VDD |
| Node X, extracted, cell alone | 2.3 fF effective (2.0 fF in schematic); wiring inside the cell adds 0.24 fF |
| Step for 0.305 mV on the gate, extracted netlist | 0.83 % |

Prediction (night plan, N6): DRC clean, held. Node capacitance 3 to 8 fF: **failed low**, 2.3 fF. The cell alone is small; the
wire from X to the inverter is not drawn yet and will dominate. Step 0.75 to 0.90 %: held.

## Residual applied to our own cell

`post_layout.sp` also simulates the cell with its stress geometry annotated. A cell with 0.30 um of diffusion, biased by a chain
whose mirror devices are simulated at the default geometry, discharges in 55.6 us instead of 36.8 us with a matched chain: a
factor of 1.5 that the unannotated flow does not see (it gives 29.0 us for both).

Design rule that follows: the 16 mirror devices of the bias chain must be drawn with the same diffusion geometry as the device
under test.
